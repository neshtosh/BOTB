Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Unsigned (“we”, “us”) is a release desk for independent artists and creators: you upload your music or videos once, and we help you publish them to platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and SoundCloud from your own accounts. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run your account, publish what you ask us to and show you how your posts are doing.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.
  • The keys that let us post to your platform accounts stay on our server and are never sent to your browser. You can disconnect any platform at any time.

1. Information we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and stored only in hashed form; we never see them.
  • Your creator handle, display name and the kind of creator you are.

Your profile

  • The bio, profile image link and website or social links you add. These appear on your public creator page.

Content you upload

  • Audio and video files, cover art and clips made from them.
  • Release details: titles, descriptions, tags, genres, release dates, track credits (such as songwriters and featured artists) and the captions you write for each platform.
  • Split records: the names, email addresses, roles and shares of collaborators you list on a release. Unsigned keeps this only as a record and never holds or pays out money. Please make sure your collaborators are happy for you to add their details.

Platforms you connect

When you connect a platform account, we store:

  • The account’s ID and name (for example your YouTube channel name).
  • The access and refresh tokens the platform gives us, so we can post on your behalf. These are kept on our server only.
  • The IDs and links of posts we publish for you, and their public statistics (such as views, likes and comments).

Fans and visitors

  • If you sign up to a creator’s updates or pre-save a release, we store your email address and share it with that creator only.
  • When someone opens a release’s smart link we add one to that release’s visit count. The count contains no personal information.

Technical information

  • Our hosting providers process IP addresses and basic request logs to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • We do not use analytics tools, advertising, or tracking cookies.

2. YouTube and other Google services

Unsigned uses YouTube API Services to publish videos and read their statistics when you connect a YouTube channel. By connecting YouTube you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access

  • youtube.upload: to upload the videos and clips you choose to publish to your channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel’s ID and name when you connect, and the view, like and comment counts of the videos we uploaded for you.

What we store and how we use it

  • Your channel ID and name, the access and refresh tokens Google issues, and the IDs, links and statistics of videos we uploaded for you.
  • We use this only to publish what you ask us to and to show you your statistics in Unsigned. We don’t use YouTube data for advertising, we don’t sell it, and we don’t share it with anyone except the service providers who host Unsigned for us (see section 4).
  • Unsigned’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove our access

  • Click Disconnect next to YouTube on the Connections page in your Unsigned studio. This deletes the tokens we hold straight away.
  • You can also revoke Unsigned’s access at any time from your Google account’s third-party connections page.
  • To have the video statistics we stored deleted as well, email us (section 9).

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and your public creator page.
  • To store your releases and publish them to the platforms you choose, at your request.
  • To show you your posts’ statistics.
  • To let fans sign up to your updates and pre-save your releases.
  • To send emails needed to run your account, such as confirming your email address.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

4. Who we share information with

  • Service providers that host Unsigned: Supabase (accounts, database and file storage) and Vercel (website and server functions). They process data only on our behalf. Their servers may be located outside your country.
  • Platforms you connect: when you publish, we send the content, captions and settings you chose to that platform. What happens to it there is covered by that platform’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • The public: your profile, public uploads and public releases are visible on your creator page and smart links. You choose what is public.
  • Creators: fans’ sign-up and pre-save emails go to the creator they signed up with.
  • Content delivery: your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the clip maker downloads its video tool from unpkg.com. These services see your IP address when your browser requests those files.
  • The law: if we are legally required to, or to protect people or the service from harm.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Storage in your browser

  • We keep your login session in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in.
  • A small flag in session storage stops a smart link visit being counted twice.
  • The clip maker’s video tool is cached by your browser after the first download.
  • In demo mode, all demo data stays in your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB and is never sent to us.

None of this is used for tracking or advertising.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account, profile and content: until you delete them, or until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Platform tokens: until you disconnect the platform, when they are deleted immediately.
  • Post links and statistics: while the related release exists, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Fan emails: until the creator removes them, or the fan asks us to delete them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, email us from the address linked to your account. We’ll respond within 30 days.

8. Security and children

Data is sent over encrypted connections, each user can only reach their own private data, and platform tokens are only accessible to our server. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Unsigned is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age to consent to data processing in your country), and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: hello@unsigned.app

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we’ll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll let account holders know by email or in the studio.
